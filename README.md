# Untitled Dice Game

A rogue-lite game about rolling dice, playing cards, and defeating enemies. You have six equipped cards, numbered 1 through 6. Each die you roll plays the card in the matching slot: roll a 3, and your third card is played.

Cards have different effects, including dealing damage, gaining shields, healing, earning coins, and applying status effects. Your choice of cards shapes what each roll can do.

## How to play

1. Choose six cards for your loadout.
2. Roll the dice on your turn. You can spend a limited number of rerolls on individual dice before accepting the results.
3. The results activate the corresponding cards. Matching dice can increase the strength of their effects.
4. Survive the enemy's turn and keep fighting until it is defeated.
5. Between battles, spend coins in the shop to buy or upgrade cards and adjust your loadout for the next fight.

The dice determine which cards you play, while your loadout and reroll choices give you ways to influence the outcome.

## Controls

- Click **Roll** to roll the dice.
- Click an individual die to reroll it while rerolls remain.
- Click **OK** to accept your current results. Using all available rerolls also accepts the results automatically.

## Running the project

Built with Unity and C#.

1. Add this project folder in Unity Hub.
2. Open it with Unity **6000.6.7f1**, the version recorded in the project settings.
3. Open `Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity` and press **Play**.
